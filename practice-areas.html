Insurance companies rarely evaluate a major case by looking only at what happened. They investigate every possible defense: who controlled the site, whether the injured person was at fault, whether a condition existed long enough to be discovered, and whether the medical evidence proves causation. Shapiro Law Offices, PLLC builds each case with those disputes in mind.
