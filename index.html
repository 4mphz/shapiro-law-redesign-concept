MEET THE ATTORNEY

Jason Shapiro, Esq

Jason Shapiro is an Ivy League–educated trial attorney who represents victims of accidents throughout the Bronx and greater New York City. He is an accomplished trial attorney, lecturer, and the author of the textbook The Lawyers’ Guide to Personal Injury Law, a comprehensive guide for plaintiffs’ personal injury attorneys.

He has been selected to Super Lawyers (2018–2020, 2023–2025) and holds Martindale-Hubbell’s AV Preeminent rating — the highest peer-review distinction for legal ability and ethical standards. His firm serves clients across Bronx, New York, Queens, Kings, Westchester, Nassau, Richmond, and Rockland Counties.
