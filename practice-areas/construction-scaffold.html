New York’s Strongest Worker Protections

New York’s Labor Law §240 (the “Scaffold Law”) and §241(6) give construction workers some of the strongest injury protections in the country, holding owners and contractors accountable for gravity-related and unsafe-condition injuries on job sites. Shapiro Law Offices, PLLC represents workers hurt in incidents involving:

  • Scaffold accidents
  • Construction accidents
  • Sidewalk bridge accidents
  • Window washing accidents
  • Ladder accidents
  • Falling objects accidents
  • Crane/forklift accidents
  • Demolition and excavation accidents

Job-site evidence changes daily, and insurers for owners and contractors begin building their defense immediately. If you or a family member was hurt on a construction site, contact Shapiro Law Offices, PLLC for a free consultation.
