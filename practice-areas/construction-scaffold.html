Scaffold & Construction Accidents

New York’s Labor Law §240 (the “Scaffold Law”) and §241(6) give construction workers some of the strongest injury protections in the country — holding owners and contractors strictly liable for gravity-related and unsafe-condition injuries on job sites. Shapiro Law Offices, PLLC has represented workers hurt in incidents involving:

  • Scaffold collapses and falls
  • Construction site accidents generally
  • Sidewalk bridge accidents
  • Window washing accidents
  • Ladder accidents
  • Falling objects accidents
  • Crane and forklift accidents
  • Demolition and excavation accidents

These cases move fast — evidence at a job site changes daily, and insurers for owners and contractors begin building their defense immediately. If you or a family member was hurt on a construction site, contact Shapiro Law Offices, PLLC for a free consultation.
