How New York Law May Apply

Drivers and owners may be liable for negligent operation. Employers and other entities may also be responsible depending on ownership, agency, employment, maintenance, and independent negligence.

Potentially Responsible Parties

  • Driver and registered owner
  • Employer or fleet operator
  • Maintenance, loading, leasing, or contracting entities where supported

Evidence That Can Make the Difference

  • Electronic data, cameras, GPS, telematics, dispatch, and route records
  • Driver qualification, hours, training, and disciplinary records
  • Maintenance, inspection, load, and repair records

Defenses We Prepare to Meet

Companies may distance themselves from the driver, minimize impact, blame roadway conditions, or argue the other vehicle entered a turn already underway. Native data and contracts should be preserved early.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why send a preservation demand quickly?

Fleet video and electronic data may be overwritten on short schedules.

Is the employer always liable?

Not always. Employment, agency, scope of work, ownership, and independent negligence must be investigated.

What insurance applies?

That depends on vehicle, ownership, use, contracts, and policies; a complete coverage search is important.
