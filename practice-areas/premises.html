Premises Accidents

Property owners, landlords, and building managers in New York have a legal duty to keep their premises reasonably safe. When they fail to, and someone gets hurt, Shapiro Law Offices, PLLC holds them accountable. We handle:

  • Sidewalk accidents
  • Snow and ice accidents
  • Stairway accidents
  • Lead paint poisoning
  • Wet floor accidents
  • Elevator and escalator accidents
  • Building accidents
  • Falls from windows
  • Dog bites
[ Premises Accidents — Illustrative Photo ]

Premises cases often turn on notice — whether the owner knew, or should have known, about the dangerous condition. Prompt investigation matters. If you were hurt on someone else’s property, contact us before evidence disappears.
