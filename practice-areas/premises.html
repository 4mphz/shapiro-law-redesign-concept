How We Analyze the Case

Depending on the location, potentially responsible parties may include owners, managing agents, tenants, contractors, public entities, and others that created or controlled the hazard.

Matters We Handle

  • Sidewalk defects
  • Snow and ice
  • Broken or wet stairs
  • Wet and slippery floors
  • Ceiling collapses and building defects
  • Lead paint, elevators, escalators, windows, and dog bites

Evidence and Preparation

  • Preserve physical, photographic, video, electronic, and documentary evidence
  • Identify every responsible person or entity and all available insurance
  • Develop the medical proof of causation, prognosis, future care, and damages
  • Prepare from the beginning for the defenses likely to be raised
