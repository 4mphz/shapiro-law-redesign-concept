Motor Vehicle Accidents

From Bronx side streets to the Grand Concourse and the highways beyond, negligent drivers cause life-changing injuries every day. Shapiro Law Offices, PLLC represents victims of:

  • Car accidents
  • Truck and tractor-trailer accidents
  • Pedestrian and bicyclist accidents
  • Motorcycle accidents
  • Bus, subway, and train accidents

New York’s no-fault insurance system and serious-injury threshold make motor vehicle claims more complicated than they first appear. Jason Shapiro has handled these cases for years and knows how to build the record insurers cannot ignore.
