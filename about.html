About The Firm

Shapiro Law Offices, PLLC is a personal injury law firm dedicated to representing victims of accidents. Founded by Jason Shapiro, the firm’s attorneys try cases — between them they have handled multi-million dollar accident matters in state and federal court for decades.

The firm focuses on all areas of accidents — scaffold and construction accidents, motor vehicle accidents, premises accidents, and medical malpractice — serving clients throughout Bronx County, New York County, Queens County, Kings County, Westchester County, Nassau County, Richmond County, and Rockland County.
