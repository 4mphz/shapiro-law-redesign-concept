About The Firm

Shapiro Law Offices, PLLC is a personal injury law firm dedicated to representing victims of accidents. Founded by Jason Shapiro, the firm’s attorneys try cases — between them they have handled multi-million dollar accident matters in state and federal court for decades.

The firm focuses on all areas of accidents — scaffold and construction accidents, motor vehicle accidents, premises accidents, and medical malpractice — serving clients throughout Bronx County, New York County, Queens County, Kings County, Westchester County, Nassau County, Richmond County, and Rockland County.

Jason Shapiro, Esq

Founder

An Ivy League–educated trial attorney, lecturer, and author of the textbook The Lawyers’ Guide to Personal Injury Law. He represents victims of accidents throughout the Bronx and greater New York City.

  • Super Lawyers selectee.
  • Martindale-Hubbell AV Preeminent® — the highest peer-review rating for legal ability and ethical standards.
  • Published author — The Lawyers’ Guide to Personal Injury Law, a comprehensive textbook discussing, in depth, the handling of all types of accident and injury-related cases.
  • Lecturer on personal injury litigation.

Hon. Ernest Buonocore

Attorney · Judge · Mediator

An experienced trial attorney, judge and mediator, handling complex litigation matters in construction accidents, premises liability, motor vehicle accidents and medical malpractice. He is adept at all aspects of litigation including trials, depositions, writing and arguing motions and appeals, and practices extensively in both state and federal courts. He has handled a variety of multi-million dollar accident cases over the last two decades.

  • Acting Village Justice, Suffern, New York.
  • J.D., Pace University School of Law.
  • B.A., State University of New York at Buffalo.
  • Longstanding member, Bronx County Bar Association.
