Meet The Founder

Jason Shapiro, Esq.

[ Attorney Photo — Jason Shapiro, Esq. ]
Founder & Trial Attorney

An Ivy League–Educated Trial Attorney

Shapiro Law Offices, PLLC is a personal injury law firm dedicated to representing victims of accidents. The firm is headed by its founder, Jason Shapiro, an accomplished trial attorney, lecturer, and author of the textbook The Lawyers’ Guide to Personal Injury Law.

The firm focuses on all areas of accident and injury law — scaffold and construction accidents, motor vehicle accidents, premises accidents, and medical malpractice — representing clients throughout Bronx County, New York County, Queens County, Kings County, Westchester County, Nassau County, Richmond County, and Rockland County.
