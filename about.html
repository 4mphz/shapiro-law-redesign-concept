Jason Shapiro, Esq.

Founder and Trial Attorney

Jason Shapiro is an Ivy League-educated trial attorney with more than 30 years of experience representing injured New Yorkers. He handles substantial construction, motor-vehicle, premises-liability, and medical-malpractice matters in state and federal court.

  • Author of The Lawyers’ Guide to Personal Injury Law, a comprehensive textbook for plaintiffs’ personal injury attorneys

  • Selected to Super Lawyers

Hon. Ernest Buonocore

Senior Trial Attorney

Hon. Ernest Buonocore brings the perspective of an experienced trial lawyer, former judge, and mediator to the firm’s serious personal injury cases. His background helps the legal team evaluate how disputed facts, witnesses, experts, and damages may be viewed in negotiation and in the courtroom.

A Trial-Focused Team
